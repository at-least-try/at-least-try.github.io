About Me

THE MOMENT IT CLICKED

I was on a morning jog with a backpack. Every stride made it hit against my back — a small, persistent annoyance. I thought: what if someone fixed this? Weeks later, I discovered a business built on that exact idea.

It struck me. The best ideas don't come from brainstorming sessions — they come from the everyday problems that frustrate the keenest observers. I decided to become one of those people who builds the solutions.

That's why I'm here. Computer Science at UofT, learning to turn frustrations into software. My rule: I only ship products I personally find value in using.
